Bhairava Foundation

Bhairava Foundation is a trust set up for the neutering, vaccination and treatment of street dogs and cats.  It also has a small shelter for 25 old, abandoned and homeless dogs. It is also involved in a vigorous campaign to neuter and vaccinate dogs and cats. Bhairava Foundation firmly believes that in order to improve the conditions of street dogs and cats, neutering is the only answer to prevent unwanted births and consequent terrible deaths on the streets due to disease, hunger and accidents.  We get around four to five dogs and occasionally cats neutered in a week. Our focus has been more on dogs since they are easier to catch. Cats are mostly feral and are very difficult to capture but we do get cats neutered whenever she is able to catch them. We get lots of calls from volunteers for help in neutering cats and dogs. These are individual volunteers who don’t have any funds to neuter or vaccinate. We sponsor surgeries for them whenever sufficient funds are available.

Besides being informed about animals that need neutering through a network of volunteers all over the city, We also drive around from street to street looking for street dogs and cats that need neutering. Besides neutering, we also get the dogs and cats vaccinated for anti rabies. The cost of neutering is Rs 3000/- for female dogs or cats and Rs 2000/- for a male dog or cat. The anti rabies vaccine is an additional Rs 300/- which gives immunity for three years. Younger dogs have less immunity and hence they are more susceptible to distemper. So, we also get the younger dogs vaccinated with the DHPPI vaccine which is an additional Rs 500/-. We need more donors to come forward to support our campaign. Bhairava Foundation is a registered trust with 80G tax exemption for donors.
